u u — Privacy Policy

Last updated 4 September 2026. This is the same text the app shows, at the consent gate and again under Settings → What happens to my photos. It is written from what the code does, not from what a policy usually says.

The short version

u u has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no trackers. Your likeness, every picture u u makes, your closet and your settings are stored on your iPhone and excluded from iCloud and computer device backups. Selected photos, your likeness and shop photos leave the phone only in the image requests described below. u u does not retain a server copy.

Your photos

You choose which photos to give u u. Apple's picker hands over only the ones you select — u u never gets access to your library. u u does not save its own copy of the photos you select. To make your likeness, it sends those photos and, if you supplied it, a plain-language description of your height and build through u u's Cloudflare Worker to OpenRouter and the Meta Muse model provider. After that request, your selected originals are not sent again: every look is made from the likeness stored on your iPhone.

Your closet

Photographs of your own clothes are different from the photographs of you, and the difference is worth stating plainly: they never leave your iPhone. You photograph a garment, or choose a picture of one. iOS cuts it out from its background on the device — the same mechanism that lifts a subject out of a photo in Apple's Photos app — and the cut-out is saved on your iPhone. Nothing about a closet piece is sent to u u, to the image service, or to any shop. Not the photograph, not the cut-out, not what you called it, not how you scored it. What it changes is what u u searches for next, and that happens on your iPhone too.

Your likeness

A photo of your face and the likeness made from it are sensitive face data. u u uses them to make pictures of you, not to identify or authenticate you. Whether a particular law calls a face image a biometric identifier depends on that law and how the image is used; u u does not make that categorical claim. u u asks you to agree before it makes a likeness. New agreements record the notice version and agreement time on your iPhone. Older installs that stored only the agreement flag keep that evidence without inventing a date. The likeness is stored on your iPhone, excluded from device backups, and sent with shop product photos each time u u makes a look or close-up.

Who processes what

The image service. OpenRouter and the Meta Muse model provider process the selected photos or likeness, shop product photos and the instructions needed to make each picture. Every request sets `provider.data_collection` to `deny`. The Muse endpoint u u currently uses is not on OpenRouter's public Zero Data Retention endpoint list, so u u does not claim that the model provider retains this data for zero time. Their handling is governed by their own terms. u u's server. A small Cloudflare Worker holds the image-service key so the app never has to. It receives each request in transit, forwards it and does not write or retain a copy of the photos, likeness or prompt. Its logs contain the number of images, prompt length, cost, duration and the image service's request ID — not the pictures or prompt text. Cloudflare also processes the network address, as any web server does. Throttling is keyed to that network address, not to a device, so devices sharing one address share the limit. Shopify. Searching for clothes sends the search words and a country to Shopify's catalog. Building a bag sends the items to that shop. Neither carries your name, your likeness or an account. The shops. Loading a product photo is a request to that shop's image server, as loading any web image is. Buying happens on their site under their terms.

What u u never does

  • No account, no email, no phone number
  • No analytics, crash reporting or advertising identifiers
  • No selling or sharing of anything with anyone for advertising
  • No payment details — u u never takes payment
  • No sending your photos to anyone except to make your pictures

Deleting

Settings → Delete my photos and likeness removes your likeness, every look, every close-up, every piece you photographed into your closet, the local consent record and every other u u record from your iPhone. It also cancels u u's pending background work and notifications. If the deletion cannot be completed, u u keeps you in Settings and tells you to retry instead of claiming success. Deletion cannot be undone. u u has no retained server copy to delete; requests already processed by OpenRouter and the model provider remain subject to their terms described above.

Children

u u is intended for people aged 12 and over. If you are under the age of majority where you live, use u u only with permission from a parent or legal guardian. u u does not knowingly build a likeness of a child under 12.
